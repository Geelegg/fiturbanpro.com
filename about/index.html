About Guy

Fit. Urban. Professional.

In high school I couldn't bench the bar. I started in engineering, switched to health science, and came out frustrated with how much of health care treats symptoms instead of people.

I worked as a PT, then in corporate life, and saw the same thing in every office: capable men running their health on scattered tactics. Fit Urban Pro is the method I built for them: one plan, every piece covered, measured every week.

Mission: the best health and performance coaching for Australia's professional men.
